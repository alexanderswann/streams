Where the data comes from

The eight stream maps are built from public data, a few agencies' own layers and some guidebooks. Here is every source, grouped by what you see on the map. Tap a source for what it is, what the maps use it for, who publishes it, its licence or terms, the credit it asks for and the maps it covers. Each map's credits line links here.

Streams, drainages and creek profiles

The stream lines and their names, sizes and colours, the drainage outlines, the drops found in the terrain and the creek profiles.

3D Elevation Program (3DEP), 1/3 arc-second elevationU.S. Geological Survey · All maps
Web
usgs.gov
What it is
The national elevation model, about 33 ft (10 m) cells, from lidar and older surveys.
On the maps
Draws every stream: water is routed over this terrain, which gives each creek its path, drainage area, gradient and order, the drops and possible waterfalls, the creek profiles, the DEM colour relief, sun and shade on the water, and the walking times.
Maps
All maps
Licence
Public domain: USGS data may be used freely, and USGS asks to be credited. Terms
Credit
U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program
National Hydrography Dataset (NHD), high resolutionU.S. Geological Survey · All maps
Web
usgs.gov
What it is
The national map of streams, rivers, lakes, springs and their names (from GNIS), at 1:24,000 or finer.
On the maps
Creek names; which creeks NHD maps as year-round or part of the year; where the routing follows mapped channels and lake outlets; the size floor (named and year-round creeks are drawn smaller); lake outlines, names, inlets and outlets; springs; mapped waterfalls.
Maps
All maps
Licence
Public domain: USGS data may be used freely, and USGS asks to be credited. Terms
Credit
U.S. Geological Survey, National Hydrography Dataset
NHDPlus Version 2U.S. Environmental Protection Agency and U.S. Geological Survey · All maps
Web
epa.gov
What it is
The national stream network at 1:100,000 with drainage areas, routing and modelled mean monthly flows (EROM).
On the maps
The drainage that rivers bring in from beyond a map's edge, lake outlets, which arm a river's name follows, and outside California the summer flow estimate (EROM's mean flow, scaled by this area's gauges).
Maps
All maps
Licence
Public domain (USGS and EPA data); USGS asks to be credited. Terms
Watershed Boundary Dataset (WBD)U.S. Geological Survey and USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service · All maps
Web
usgs.gov
What it is
The national map of hydrologic units, the land that drains to each part of the stream network, nested from large regions down to small watersheds.
On the maps
Which of NHD's downloads each map reads: NHD comes in 4-digit hydrologic units, and a map reads those whose outlines meet its boxes.
Maps
All maps
Licence
Public domain: USGS data may be used freely, and USGS asks to be credited. Terms
Credit
U.S. Geological Survey, Watershed Boundary Dataset
NLCD 2021 impervious cover, 30 mU.S. Geological Survey, served by the Multi-Resolution Land Characteristics Consortium (MRLC) · All maps
Web
mrlc.gov
What it is
The National Land Cover Database's share of each 30 m cell covered by roofs, roads and pavement, for 2021.
On the maps
Creeks a city has put in sewers: an unnamed line NHD doesn't map, through ground 40 % or more paved over, with hardly any open waterway along it and no trout water near, isn't drawn. Drawn all the same: the outlet NHD maps from a lake or pond of 2.5 acres or more, and a gap under 1,640 ft (500 m) with the creek drawn above and below.
Maps
All maps
Licence
Public domain (USGS data); MRLC's service states no fees and no access constraints. USGS asks to be credited. Terms
Credit
U.S. Geological Survey, National Land Cover Database (NLCD) 2021
