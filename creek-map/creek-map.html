      The Deep Creek video

      • “Deep in the San Bernardino Mountains” (Anglin’ Man, filmed 3/21/2026) names no creek or road, so this is worked out from clues.
      • The description puts it on a north-slope creek with deep granite pools and wild rainbows. The angler is following up a Nov 2024 video of the creek just downstream.
      • In that 2024 video he says he used to drive down this road, that it has been closed for several years, and that it used to be overrun with 4x4s. The Forest Service closed the Aztec Falls / Splinters Cabin area for overcrowding.
      • The opening shot shows a rough track over granite ledges on an open hillside. That best fits FR 3N34’s rocky “very horrible” 4x4 grade down to the Deep Creek crossing. FR 2N26Y is paved, and 3N34C runs along the canyon floor.
      • Forest Order 05-12-52-25-02 (to June 7, 2027) closes Deep Creek and 50 ft either side from the Hooks Creek confluence down to Devil’s Hole. It also closes 3N34C and the Splinters Cabin area to everyone, and 2N26Y to vehicles. The upstream stretch he fished in 2026 is outside that zone. Confirm with the Mountaintop Ranger District (909-382-2790).

      Before you go

      Check the current CDFW special regulations for the exact creek: some stretches are zero-limit with barbless artificial lures only, and some are closed to protect arroyo toads or native trout. Check Angeles NF alerts for road and area closures, and bring an Adventure Pass or equivalent for parking.
